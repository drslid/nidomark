Nidomark is a browser extension that rebuilds a whole bookmark collection into a clear tree. A small multilingual AI model runs inside the browser: it proposes a place for every bookmark, you review the new tree, and nothing changes until you apply it.

## What it does

- **Files everything you choose.** The whole collection by default, the shortcuts of the bookmarks bar included, or only the folders you tick. Every bookmark gets a place in the new tree: nothing is left where it was.
- **Puts your favourite sites first.** The bar opens with the icons of the sites you visit most, such as your mailbox or your video site, without their names.
- **Uses a small set of folders.** About a dozen top-level folders, such as 🏠 Home, 💻 Tech, 🎬 Leisure or 🏛️ Everyday life, the most used first, hold precise subfolders such as 📺 Streaming or 📡 Tech news, created only when there are enough bookmarks for them. A big category is split once more, by site or by subject: three levels at most.
- **Keeps the folders that make sense.** A folder whose bookmarks belong together, such as "Wedding 2025", is recreated with its subfolders. Catch-all folders such as "Misc", "To sort" or "Imported" are emptied.
- **Reads a page when the title is not enough**, sets apart the sites that no longer answer, and leaves duplicates out.
- **Explains every choice**, and files in "❓ To check" the bookmarks it cannot place with confidence, with a suggestion when it leans somewhere.
- **Learns from you.** When you file a bookmark elsewhere in the review, the next sorts send its site there too.

## How it decides

Each bookmark is assessed from the most certain evidence to the least:

1. an address only reachable at home (a NAS, a box, Home Assistant) goes to 🖥️ Home services;
2. a site that no longer answers goes to 🚫 Unreachable sites;
3. adult content always goes to 🔞 Porn;
4. a site you filed yourself in an earlier sort goes where you put it;
5. a known site or a typical address is filed for sure: more than 1,000 sites are recognised;
6. the local model compares the title with typical bookmarks of each category, and with the bookmarks of your collection it already recognised;
7. when that is not clear, the page itself is read, and the home page of its site if the page says too little;
8. if the evidence is still too weak, the bookmark goes to "❓ To check", with the reason.

[How sorting works](/reference/how-it-works/) gives the details, and [Categories](/reference/categories/) lists every folder Nidomark can create.

## What it never does

- It never sends your bookmarks anywhere. The model runs offline; the only network requests are the bookmarked pages themselves, without your cookies, and only if you allow it.
- It never changes anything before you click **File**.
- It never deletes a bookmark. The new tree is made of copies; your previous tree is moved, as it was, into a dated backup in Other bookmarks, and one click undoes the whole sort.

  bookmarks**.
</Aside>
