## Status

Nidomark is an **alpha**: version 0.5, for Chrome on a computer, installed in developer mode from the [Releases page](https://github.com/drslid/Nidomark/releases). Its sorting was tuned on a fictitious collection and is now being tested on real ones.

What works today: recreating a whole collection in a new tree, with quick access icons of the most visited sites first and a dozen top-level folders with categories inside, three levels at most; reading pages when titles are not enough; setting dead links, duplicates and adult content apart; a review where everything can be changed (folders at every level, drag and drop, renaming, removing, undo) with suggestions for every bookmark to check and a new analysis of any folder; partial sorts that add to the folders already there; keeping the previous tree in a dated backup with one-click undo; learning from your corrections; emoji folder names, in 21 languages.

## Roadmap

1. **Sorting on real collections**: corrections from the first users, more known sites and examples.
2. **Chrome Web Store**: a public listing, installed and updated like any extension.
3. **Firefox and Edge**: builds for both browsers, from the same code.
4. **Later**: cleaning targeted visits from the browsing history, and a password-protected vault for private bookmarks.

<Aside>
  Dates are not promised: each step ships when it is tested. Follow the [releases](https://github.com/drslid/Nidomark/releases) to know when one lands.
</Aside>

## Take part

- **Report a misfiled bookmark or a bug** in the [issues](https://github.com/drslid/Nidomark/issues), with the address, where it went and where you expected it.
- **Report a vulnerability** privately, as the [security policy](https://github.com/drslid/Nidomark/blob/main/SECURITY.md) describes.
- **Contribute code** following the [contributing guide](https://github.com/drslid/Nidomark/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md); [Build from source](/project/development/) gets you started.
