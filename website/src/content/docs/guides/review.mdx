## Read the map

The folders are grouped under the place they will be created in, as in Chrome:

- **Bookmarks bar**: ⚡ **Quick access**, the icons that open the bar, then the top-level folders, the most used first. Under each name, its subfolders.
- **Other bookmarks**: ❓ **To check**, in amber, with the bookmarks Nidomark could not place with confidence; 🔞 Porn and 🚫 Unreachable sites when there are any.
- **Not recreated**: the **Duplicates**, copies of a page kept elsewhere. They stay in the backup.

Each folder shows the icons of a few of its bookmarks and how many it holds, subfolders included.

The strip at the top previews your new bookmarks bar: the quick access icons, then the folders as Chrome will show them. Click a bookmark there to show its name, or to show it as an icon again.

## Open a folder

Click a folder to open it in the side panel: first its own bookmarks, then each of its subfolders. Each bookmark shows its site and, when it differs from the rest of the folder, the reason it goes there: a known site, a typical address, what its page says, a site you filed yourself, or one of your folders recreated as it is.

- **Move a bookmark** with its move button, or drag it onto another folder: every folder that can take it lights up while you drag. The move menu follows the new tree, subfolders included, and offers Quick access too.
- **Rename a folder** in the panel header, or a subfolder in its heading; change an emoji with the tile next to the name. Your names are kept for the next sorts.
- **Don't create** a subfolder: its bookmarks go to the folder above it. **Create this folder** brings it back.
- **Create a folder of your own** with **New folder**, at the bottom of the map or in the move menu.
- **Accept a suggestion**: in To check, a bookmark the model leans to place somewhere shows a suggestion; one click files it there. The move menu lists the suggestion first.
- **Open a page** to see it for yourself: bookmarks to check and unreachable sites have an open button.
- **Keep a cleaner title or the original one** from the move menu, when Nidomark proposes a cleaner title.

When you file a bookmark elsewhere than proposed, Nidomark remembers it: the next sorts send that page, and the other pages of its site, to the same category. Undoing the sort forgets it.

Everything works with the keyboard: Tab moves between folders, Enter opens one, Escape closes the panel, and the move menu replaces dragging. Moves are announced to screen readers.

## Apply

The bar at the bottom always says that nothing has changed yet.

- **File _N_ bookmarks** creates the new tree, then moves your previous tree, as it was, into a dated backup. _N_ counts every bookmark recreated; duplicates are not.
- **Restore proposal** brings back every choice Nidomark made, undoing your changes in the review.
- The back arrow leaves the review without changing anything, after asking: the analysis would have to run again.
- If your bookmarks change in Chrome during the review, Nidomark asks you to refresh the proposal before applying.

Applying ends on a summary with an **Undo** button: see [Backup and undo](/guides/backup-and-undo/).
