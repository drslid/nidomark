Nidomark files bookmarks into a small, fixed set of folders. A top-level folder holds the categories of its group; a category becomes a subfolder only when enough bookmarks need it, and a group with a single category takes the category's name. A big category is split once more, by site or by subject. Names follow the language of your browser: English or French.

Besides the categories, the new tree can hold ⚡ Quick access (icons at the start of the bar), 🗂️ My folders (your projects that mix subjects), and, in Other bookmarks, ❓ To check and 🧩 Browser links (bookmarklets, browser pages and files).

## Your own folder names count

A folder of yours named after a category or a group, in any of the 21 languages of Nidomark, is recognised: its bookmarks join that category, and the new folder takes your name for it: "Séries" or "Movies" for Streaming, "Recettes" or "Recipes" for Cooking, "Loisirs" for Leisure. Nidomark also recognises the folders it created, even shown as an emoji alone, and keeps the names you gave them.

Something is filed in the wrong category, or a site you use is missing? [Open an issue](https://github.com/drslid/Nidomark/issues) with the address, where it went and where you expected it.
